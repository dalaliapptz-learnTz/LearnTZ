## 1. Executive Overview

Under the 2023 Education and Training Policy and the 2023 Primary Education Curriculum Framework (*Curriculum for Primary Education Standard I - VI*, ISBN `978-9987-09-852-1`), primary education in Tanzania spans six standards (Darasa la I hadi la VI). 

The curriculum transitions from **Foundational Learning Areas (Kusoma, Kuandika, Kuhesabu na Afya)** in Standards I and II into **Seven Compulsory Subjects and One Elective Subject** in Standards III through VI.

This inventory provides the comprehensive register of all official subjects, curriculum versions, period allocations, source documents, and implementation statuses across LearnTZ.

## 3. Structural Breakdown by Standard and Subject

### A. Standards I and II (Foundational Tier)
Standards I and II focus on foundational literacies and personal development:
- **Weekly Total Periods:** 24 periods of 30 minutes each.
- **Learning Area 1 (Literacy & Communication):** Kusoma na Kuandika (Reading and Writing) — 10 periods/week.
- **Learning Area 2 (Numeracy):** Kuhesabu (Mathematics/Numeracy) — 6 periods/week.
- **Learning Area 3 (Health & Environment):** Afya na Mazingira — 4 periods/week.
- **Learning Area 4 (Creative Arts & Physical Activities):** Sanaa na Michezo — 4 periods/week.

### B. Standards III and IV (Lower Primary Subject-Based Tier)
- **Weekly Total Periods:** 38 periods of 40 minutes each (Compulsory: 35 periods; Elective: 3 periods).
- **Subjects:**
  1. Kiswahili: 7 periods/week
  2. English Language: 7 periods/week (**Verified: 244 periods in Std III, 234 periods in Std IV**)
  3. Mathematics: 6 periods/week
  4. Science and Technology: 5 periods/week
  5. Geography and Environment: 4 periods/week
  6. History of Tanzania and Ethics: 4 periods/week
  7. Arts and Sports: 5 periods/week
  8. Elective (Chinese / French / Arabic): 3 periods/week

### C. Standards V and VI (Upper Primary Subject-Based Tier)
- **Weekly Total Periods:** 36 periods of 40 minutes each (Compulsory: 33 periods; Elective: 3 periods).
- **Subjects:**
  1. Kiswahili: 6 periods/week
  2. English Language: 6 periods/week (**Verified: 224 periods in Std V, 150 periods in Std VI**)
  3. Mathematics: 6 periods/week
  4. Science and Technology: 5 periods/week
  5. Geography and Environment: 4 periods/week
  6. History of Tanzania and Ethics: 4 periods/week
  7. Arts and Sports: 5 periods/week
  8. Elective (Chinese Language): 3 periods/week
