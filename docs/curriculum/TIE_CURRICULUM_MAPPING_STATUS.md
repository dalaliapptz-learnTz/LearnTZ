## 3. Seven Required Matrix Fields Audit

For every subject and standard, all seven official matrix columns defined in TIE syllabi are rigorously mapped:

1. **Main Competence (Umahiri Mkuu):** Overarching capability domain (e.g., *Kuwasiliana katika miktadha mbalimbali*, *Demonstrate mastery of elementary mathematical skills*).
2. **Specific Competence (Umahiri Mahususi):** Alphanumeric coded granular outcome statement (e.g., `1.1`, `1.2`, `2.1`).
3. **Learning Activities (Shughuli za Ujifunzaji):** Lettered learner actions (`(a)`, `(b)`, `(c)`).
4. **Suggested Teaching & Learning Methods (Mbinu za Ufundishaji na Ujifunzaji):** Pedagogical procedures (e.g., role play, demonstration, problem solving, group discussion).
5. **Assessment Criteria (Vigezo vya Upimaji):** Concrete performance benchmarks.
6. **Suggested Teaching & Learning Resources (Zana na Vifaa):** Concrete classroom realia, charts, texts, and media.
7. **Number of Periods (Idadi ya Vipindi):** Exact period counts allocated per competence.
